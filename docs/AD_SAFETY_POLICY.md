# PayVoice — Ad-First Policy-Safe Monetization Framework

## Core Principle
> **Payment utility always has priority over entertainment and monetization.** A payment alert must never be delayed, hidden, interrupted, or covered by an advertisement, game, animation, modal, or notification from the entertainment area.

---

## 1. Strictly Allowed Placements
PayVoice limits ad units to transparent, non-deceptive placements:
1. `home_feed`: A clearly labelled **"Sponsored Demo"** card rendered below core merchant information (metrics, sound readiness, and quick actions).
2. `business_tips`: A clearly labelled banner in the Merchant Growth & Security Tips section.
3. `play_hub`: A clearly labelled sponsored card rendered in the PayVoice Play hub between game cards (never inside an active game session).
4. `discover_offers`: Clearly labelled partner offers within merchant business settings.

---

## 2. Strictly Forbidden Placements & Dark Patterns
Under the `AdPolicyGuard` enforcement engine, ads are **NEVER** placed:
- On payment alert, payment success, or payment failure screens.
- Inside or over audio/voice controls (e.g. volume slider, chime selector, voice preview).
- Inside active mini-gameplay (e.g. while solving Brain Spark, during Memory Match card grid, inside Word Builder tiles).
- Adjacent to primary action buttons where accidental taps might occur.
- Over UPI QR payment displays or counter stand representations.
- In login, registration, or OTP verification forms.
- In any location that disguises an advertisement as system or navigation UI.

---

## 3. Anti-Deception & User Protection Rules
- **No Income Promises**: We never promise merchants income, prizes, or cash payouts for viewing ads.
- **No Incentivized Clicks**: We never award badges, streaks, or points for ad clicks.
- **No Click Loops**: Ads do not force countdown timers or auto-refresh rapidly.
- **Opt-Out Control**: Merchants have access to **Ad Preferences** where they can disable personalized recommendations.
- **Ad-Free Proposed Tier**: Merchants upgrading to the proposed Business Premium demo tier experience an interface with 100% of partner advertisements disabled.
